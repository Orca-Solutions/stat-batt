# Remaining control feasibility — October 2, 2026

This bounded follow-up inspected existing code, the original local diagnostic JSON, and current primary upstream references. It performed no hardware writes, shortcut executions, privileged operations, or helper installation.

## Finding

The next plausible backend is **adapter inhibition through CHIE**, suitable for deliberate discharge. It is not an independent charge gate preserving wall power. There is no newly established route to custom charge hold or temperature-based charge hold on this Mac.

The local report at `/private/tmp/statbatt-v1-hardware-probe.json`, generated `2026-10-02T17:45:23Z`, confirms Mac16,13 / arm64 / macOS 27.0.1 / 26A434 / mBoot-20457.1.29. CHIE metadata is one byte, `hex_`, attributes `D4`, with successful read and baseline `00`. CHTE and CH0C returned SMC result 132; CH0J returned permission denial. No later keys were attempted. These are read-only observations, not writable capability.

The pinned BSD [Battery Toolkit power source](https://github.com/mhaeuser/Battery-Toolkit/blob/ed3adf103abfdad53223ce6f0a764ae7163c385b/Libraries/SMCComm%2BPower.swift) assigns CHIE `08` to disable the adapter and `00` to re-enable it. Thus there is a concrete candidate operation and inverse, with a matching local readable baseline. The source alone does not prove the inverse succeeds on this hardware.

[batt PR 154](https://github.com/charlie0129/batt/pull/154), merged September 24, reports successful adapter control on an M5 MacBook Air with firmware 20457.1.29, including restoration on disable and recovery after daemon SIGKILL. This is encouraging same-firmware evidence, not exact-target qualification. Adapter cycling uses the battery while plugged in and cannot satisfy the spec's AC-preserving custom band or thermal pause. No GPL implementation was copied.

[batt issue 152](https://github.com/charlie0129/batt/issues/152) reports newer firmware gating charge keys and smart-battery writes even as root. Its private-entitlement explanation is upstream investigation; local failures are consistent with it but do not independently prove its cause. A privileged helper does not by itself solve this barrier.

A focused Apple documentation search found no public arbitrary charge-gate or adapter-inhibit setter. Apple's documented [Charge Limit](https://support.apple.com/en-au/102338) is 80–100% and may occasionally permit full charging. It cannot implement an arbitrary lower/upper band or immediate thermal pause. This is a bounded search finding, not proof that no other interface exists.

## Smallest useful qualification increment

1. Prepare an independently reviewed, fixed-key experiment executable, separate from the public app. Permit only CHIE and the exact documented encodings; reject unexpected fingerprint/layout/baseline and permission denial. No arbitrary key, byte, shell, or privilege interface.
2. First qualify the restoration operation alone while baseline is already `00`, confirming transport, SMC result, exact readback and continued external supplying source. This tests write permission and the restore path without intentionally cutting input. It is still a hardware write and was not performed in this read-only task.
3. Before any `08` experiment, establish a credible emergency recovery procedure for this exact target. The prior report explicitly lacks one if both the primary process and its guardian cannot restore `00`. Do not claim reboot or unplug/replug clears this latch without evidence. An independently prepared restore-only process and a short deadline reduce process-failure risk but do not prove firmware recovery.
4. Once that gap is resolved and the concrete experiment is authorized, qualify a short, supervised inhibition/restoration cycle with adequate reserve, fresh telemetry, stable adapter input, and sleep prevented only for the bounded trial. Capture actual supplying source/current and restoration, not merely byte readback. Restore on every exit/error and report ambiguous results as unresolved.
5. Only after basic effect/inverse qualification, build the owner with durable intent and restoration-first recovery, serialized sleep/policy operations, stale-sample and reserve exits. Qualify UI quit, worker death, sleep, wake, unplug, timeout and reboot cancellation individually before exposing discharge.

Proposed concrete trial envelope: one inhibition only, five seconds maximum, at least 50% battery, nominal thermal pressure, owner present, and no concurrent charging controller. Start the independent restore-only guardian before any inhibition; it must acknowledge a separate SMC connection and its immutable `00` operation. Both processes use monotonic deadlines, and the guardian attempts restoration at the deadline or parent loss. The primary process restores sooner on any unexpected read, source state or process error. Neither process repeats inhibition. Readback and observed external supply must confirm recovery before marking success. This is a qualification design, not a ready-to-run command or a claim that two processes eliminate shared OS/firmware failure.

Recommended next decision: prepare and review this fixed experiment as source, beginning with the restoration-only stage. Do not put experimental actuation in the public package. Before running inhibition, resolve the emergency-recovery uncertainty or explicitly revisit the lab's acceptance boundary with the owner; do not imply a guardian proves a hardware reset path.

[batt PR 159](https://github.com/charlie0129/batt/pull/159), merged September 27, repaired a real sleep/runtime adapter-mode race. It supports serializing ownership and lifecycle transitions from the outset.

The existing `PolicyReducer` already models these release conditions and separates hold from adapter inhibition. Passing its tests cannot establish real actuation or restoration. Filling the current helper stub or marking capabilities verified would not complete this work.

## Source-only bounded disconnect packet

The enabled-state administrator test later passed (recorded by the coordinating task). That establishes `00` write permission/readback only. The next prepared source adds a fixed `08` worker and a fixed `00` restoration path accepting baseline `00` or `08`. New modes remain disabled independently in Swift, C, and the new supervisor; the supervisor has no accepted binary hash. No disconnect execution is enabled by this preparation.

`apps/statbatt/scripts/adapter-disconnect-trial.py` prepares a separate root guardian session. It persists intent, owns the worker's start gate, waits for worker readiness and an idle-sleep assertion, and requests restoration after two seconds or earlier on parent loss/worker exit. Before launching restoration it kills and reaps the inhibition writer: restoring while a surviving writer can later issue `08` would be a race. A writer that cannot be reaped is explicitly unresolved; the guardian does not falsely claim restoration. Restoration bypasses reserve/source/thermal admission because those conditions must not prevent enabling adapter input, but retains exact target, metadata and known baseline checks. Success requires `00` readback and externally supplying source; no automatic repeated inhibition exists.

The deadline is a software restoration-request target, **not a guaranteed maximum hardware disconnection**. If kernel calls hang, either process is killed, sleep is forced, or firmware ignores restoration, the bound can be exceeded. The idle-sleep assertion does not prevent explicit sleep, lid closure, shutdown or kernel failure. Thus the requested three-second bound and no-sleep condition are not yet hardware-qualified promises.

Primary-source recovery research: Apple's [Mac battery won't charge guidance](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/mac-help/-mh29198/mac) recommends restarting an Apple-silicon Mac for SMC-related charging issues. Apple's [Mac won't turn on guidance](https://support.apple.com/en-ca/102623) distinguishes Intel SMC reset from Apple silicon. Neither documents CHIE latch clearing, Mac16,13, or firmware 20457.1.29. No exact-target physical recovery procedure was established. Unplug/replug, restart, and power-button shutdown therefore cannot be advertised as proved emergency recovery for this experiment.

The packet stays source-only pending independent review and resolution of that recovery boundary. Software tests mock all process/hardware work and establish guardian ordering/parent-loss behavior only. The existing enabled-state release executable was not rebuilt or replaced during this preparation; no hardware write was run.

Source review hardening: a guardian-owned sleep-assertion process now spans writer termination and the restoration attempt; the worker's assertion is additional. Journal failures after gate intent are recorded best-effort and cannot skip the restore call. Operator output includes the guardian's structured result, with file-backed guardian output to avoid an undrained pipe deadlock. Initial intent preserves a fresh (at most five minutes), exact-target prior inspection report, its SHA-256/timestamp, exact worker argv and explicit invocation authorization; arming is a separate durable record. Restoration requires a fresh observed adapter-source sample acquired after the write returned. Disconnect evidence remains inconclusive unless a fresh battery-source observation is obtained; `08` readback alone is not electrical proof. Host acquisition timestamps do not establish exact electrical transition latency. Six targeted Swift tests and five mocked guardian tests passed; these establish software behavior only.
