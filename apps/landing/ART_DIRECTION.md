# StatBatt art direction

Visual thesis: calm, precise battery insight, expressed through forest-green typography, generous white space, an emerald glass battery sculpture, and a small floating illustrative menu panel. The landing page should feel like a thoughtful native Mac utility rather than a charging-performance promise.

An asset-only art designer agent collaborated with implementation and an independent product reviewer. No human contractor was hired and no purchase was made.

## Hero asset

Generated once with the built-in imagegen tool and visually inspected. The artwork is original stylized concept art, not a real battery photograph or product screenshot. The JPEG in `dist/assets/battery-sculpture.jpg` is a browser-optimized encoding of the generated 1254 × 1254 PNG. The website also supplies its own original code-native battery favicon and illustrative history chart.

Lighting/mood: diffuse soft studio lighting, delicate soft shadows, restrained emerald reflections, subtle glass caustics, luminous without excessive glow.

Constraints: no text, numbers, letters, labels, UI, logos, Apple logos, laptop, wires, watermark, particles, extra objects, or cropped object. Exactly one original artwork.
